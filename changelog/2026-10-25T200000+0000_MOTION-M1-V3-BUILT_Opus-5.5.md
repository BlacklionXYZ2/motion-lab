## What v3 is (motion-lab `versions/v3`, copied from v2)

- **Quality filter** (`motionlab/datasets/quality.py`, config `"clean_data": true`).
  - Measuring the limits found broken CMU frames: about 1.8% of CMU's training frames have a
    hand, head or foot about 180° from rest, often for a whole take (17_02's right hand, all
    3,181 frames). LAFAN1 and 100STYLE have none.
  - Frames past generous sanity limits (the M0 placeholders plus 30°) are cut out with 10 frames
    either side. Pieces under 3 s are dropped.
  - **Duplicates:** CMU's own archive stores 361 takes twice under two subjects, byte for byte
    (102 = 78, 122 = 83, 105 = 91 and others; checked in `allasfamc.zip`). In v1 and v2 the same
    motion could sit in two splits. One copy remains.
  - In total 4.4% of frames are removed (`outputs/quality-v3.txt`). v2's converted data is copied
    unchanged; the filter runs as it loads.
- **Limits from the data** (`scripts/joint_limits.py` → `motionlab/assets/joint_limits.json`):
  - per joint, measured from the cleaned training data with left and right together:
    - a box on the rotation vector (0.05th to 99.95th percentile per axis);
    - the angle from rest (99.95th percentile);
  - the hips' height range: 0.06 to 1.96 m. That's too wide to catch v2's hips spikes (climbing
    and jumps reach it), so longer rollouts have to handle them.
- **Limit losses** (`m1.py`): linear past the limits and 0 inside, summed over joints, with
  weight 1 for joints and 2 for the hips.
- **Curriculum:** rollouts of 1, 8, 32, 64 and then 128 ticks of the model's own output. The
  last two phases use windows of 104 and 168 ticks. Validation uses the last phase.
- **Evaluation:** adds the share of joint-frames outside the measured limits, overall and per
  joint, and keeps the placeholder measure. `evaluate_m1.py --clean` tests an earlier model on
  v3's test set.
- **Configs:** `combined.json` (v3's run), and `combined-17m.json` (the size test on v3's setup;
  not run).

## Measured

- **Tests:** 59 passed (v2's 53 plus 6 new: the filter, the limits, the gradients and the
  curriculum's windows). Ruff clean.
- **Training speed** (RX 9070 XT, batch 128): 0.65 s per step at window 72, 0.93 at 104, 1.48 at
  168. That makes about 9.5 h for 40,000 steps.
- **Smoke run:** 40 steps through the real script, ending in the 128-tick phase: validation,
  checkpoints and state all written (`runs/smoke-v3`).
- **v2's final model on v3's cleaned test set** (`outputs/eval-v2-final-on-v3-test.json`):
  - outside the measured limits: 21% of joint-frames (forearms 51 to 75%, hands 34 to 56%,
    toes 28 to 57%); the recordings: 0.4%;
  - beyond the placeholders: 16.6%;
  - energy 0.10 → 0.06 rad/s (recordings 1.04);
  - error at 0.5 / 1 / 2 s: 9.6° / 11.3° / 15.3°.
  - Not comparable with v2's own report (12.1%), which used a different test set and seeds.
