# M1: the motion prior's training pipeline

*Moved here from `docs/M1.md` on 2026-10-25, when each model version became
self-contained. Copied into v3 on 2026-10-25; commands run from `versions\v3`. Its history
sections cover v0 to v2 too; their own folders hold what they produced.*

## What v3 changes (2026-10-25, Opus 5.5)

v2 drifted, over 60 s free runs, into limb poses no recording has (forearms, hands, toes), and
its hips spiked. Three changes, everything else as v2:

1. **Quality filter** (`datasets/quality.py`, `"clean_data": true`). Measuring the limits found
   broken CMU frames: a hand, head or foot turned about 180° from rest, often for a whole take.
   Frames past generous sanity limits (the M0 placeholders + 30°) are cut out with 10 frames
   either side, and pieces under 3 s are dropped. CMU's archive also stores 361 takes twice under
   two subjects (102 = 78, 122 = 83, 105 = 91 and others, byte for byte), which put the same
   motion in two splits; one copy remains. In total 4.4% of frames go
   (`outputs/quality-v3.txt`).
2. **Limit losses** (`m1.joint_limits`, `scripts/joint_limits.py`, asset
   `assets/joint_limits.json`). Each joint's range is measured from the cleaned training data,
   left and right together: a box on the rotation vector (0.05th to 99.95th percentile per axis,
   so a knee or a toe bends one way only) and the angle from rest (99.95th percentile). The
   hips height has a range too. The losses are linear past the limits and 0 inside, summed over
   joints, because a toe is light and averaged away in every other loss.
3. **Longer rollouts:** the curriculum goes on to 64 and then 128 ticks (about 2 s) of the
   model's own output, with windows of 104 and 168 ticks for those phases; validation uses the
   last phase. About 9.5 h for 40,000 steps (measured: 0.65, 0.93 and 1.48 s per step).

The evaluation adds the share of joint-frames outside the measured limits, overall and per
joint, keeping the placeholder measure so v1 and v2 compare. `evaluate_m1.py --clean` tests an
earlier version's model on v3's cleaned test set: v2's final model is outside the limits in 21%
of joint-frames (forearms 51 to 75%, hands 34 to 56%, toes 28 to 57%), the recordings in 0.4%
(`outputs/eval-v2-final-on-v3-test.json`).

**Not changed:** the low energy (about 10× calmer than the data). That needs conditioning (the
keywords) and possibly probabilistic output.

## Run it

```powershell
..\..\.venv\Scripts\python scripts\build_lafan1.py                                  # once: data/processed/lafan1
..\..\.venv\Scripts\python scripts\train_m1.py configs\m1\baseline.json             # about 3.5 h on the RX 9070 XT
..\..\.venv\Scripts\python scripts\train_m1.py --resume runs\baseline                # carry on after a pause
..\..\.venv\Scripts\python scripts\evaluate_m1.py runs\baseline\best.pt             # 60 s rollouts, test subject
..\..\.venv\Scripts\python -m pytest -q
```

**Pausing and resuming.**
- **To pause:** press Ctrl+C, or create an empty file named `STOP` in the run's folder
  (`runs\<name>\STOP`), which works for a run with no window. The run saves `state.pt` and
  stops; the file is renamed `STOP.handled`.
- **To resume:** `--resume runs\<name>` carries on exactly. Tested: a run paused and resumed
  ended with the same weights as one run straight through (difference 0.0).
- **Saving:** state is also saved every 250 steps (`checkpoint_every`), so a crash loses at most
  that much.
- **Starting from a trained model:** `--init-from <checkpoint>` starts a new run from that
  model's weights and input scaling.
- **Overwriting:** a new run won't overwrite a folder that already has one.

## The parts

**Data** ([data/README.md](../data/README.md)):
- `datasets/bvh.py` reads BVH.
- `datasets/lafan1.py` retargets LAFAN1 onto the canonical skeleton and resamples it to 60 Hz.
- `datasets/contacts.py` marks foot contacts.
- `datasets/m1_data.py` handles splits, window sampling, augmentation, motion states and
  normalisation.

- **Retargeting.**
  - LAFAN1's 22 joints map one to one onto the canonical skeleton, but its joint frames point
    every bone along local +X. A constant per-joint alignment fixes that:
    - the hips and every bone direction come from the BVH offsets (exact);
    - twist about each bone comes from the mean of each take's first-frame T-pose, with its
      facing removed.
  - Checked on every take: retargeted bones are within 0.0003° of LAFAN1's own.
- **Scale.**
  - The root is scaled by her rest hips height over the performers' standing hips height
    (0.895 / 0.920 = 0.973). Planted ankles then sit at 0.09 to 0.10 m on flat ground; her rest
    ankle height is 0.092 m.
  - Scaling by leg length instead put her hips 12 cm too low.
- **Contacts:** a foot or toe joint slower than 0.35 m/s is planted (speed, so a foot on a box
  counts).
- **Splits by performer:** subjects 1 to 3 train (619,540 frames), subject 4 validates
  (193,970), subject 5 tests (LAFAN1's benchmark convention).
- **Augmentation:** every window is mirrored left/right with probability ½ and turned to a random
  facing.

**100STYLE and CMU** (`datasets/retarget.py`, `datasets/style100.py`, `datasets/cmu.py`,
`scripts/build_datasets.py`):
- **Shared retarget:** a profile names, for each canonical joint, the source joint whose world
  rotation it takes and where it sits. Source joints not named (100STYLE's fourth chest joint,
  CMU's upper neck) fold into their neighbours. Both sources stand in a T-pose with world-aligned
  frames at rest. Each bone is then swung, per frame, onto the source bone's actual direction:
  without that, folding CMU's two neck bones lost up to 23°. Every bone matches its source to
  0.0°.
- **CMU's format** (ASF/AMC): each bone's world rotation is `G_parent · C · M · C⁻¹`. Each
  performer is scaled from their own rest skeleton, and each take is placed on the floor from its
  planted ankles.
- **CMU frame rates:** 512 takes are 60 fps, not 120, and BABEL's durations show which ones.
- **BABEL:** its labels are attached to 1,902 CMU takes, kept with each clip for intents later.
- **Splits:** 100STYLE (one performer) by style; CMU by performer; a tenth each for validation
  and test.
- **Results:**
  - 100STYLE: 810 takes, 22.1 h; planted ankles median 0.088 m; standing hips 0.878 m.
  - CMU: 2,503 takes, 10.8 h; planted ankles median 0.110 m; standing hips 0.877 m.
- **Combined training** (`configs/m1/combined.json`):
  - 100STYLE weighted 0.3 so its walking doesn't dominate;
  - data kept in system memory at half precision, and only batches go to the GPU;
  - 40,000 steps at about 0.66 s per step: about 7.3 hours, pausable.

**Bandai Namco** (downloaded, not built): no take starts in a T-pose and its joint frames are
bone-aligned, so it needs a different calibration (planned).

**AMASS** (`datasets/amass.py`, `scripts/build_amass.py`; ready for when AMASS is downloaded):
- **Format:** AMASS's SMPL-H files hold axis-angle rotations in a Z-up world. SMPL's 22 body
  joints map one to one onto the canonical skeleton (the hands are dropped), and its rest frames
  are axis-aligned like ours, so only the world is turned to Y-up.
- **Checked against motion-capture markers** in AMASS's official sample (`data/amass/sample/`,
  `scripts/check_amass_sample.py`, and a test that runs whenever the file is present):
  - shins, upper arms, forearms and left/right agree within 2 to 12° (markers sit on the skin);
  - the head is a constant 26° off (head-marker slope or SMPL's rest head, not a mapping
    error);
  - nothing is flipped.
- **Root, without the licensed body model:**
  - **scale:** fitted so planted feet stay still while the root travels; 1.0 when a take
    barely moves;
  - **height:** an offset that puts planted ankles at her rest ankle height.
  - SMPL's pelvis pivot is a constant 21 cm horizontal offset from `trans`; it doesn't change
    root velocity, which is all training uses.
- **Not corrected:** SMPL's rest pose differs a little from hers, by a few degrees per bone.
  Fixing it exactly needs SMPL's joint positions from the body model.
- **Layout and split:** `build_amass.py` expects AMASS's own layout,
  `data/amass/<dataset>/<subject>/*_poses.npz`. Splits follow AMASS's paper: HumanEva,
  MPI_HDM05, SFU and MPI_mosh validate; Transitions_mocap and SSM_synced test; the rest train.
  Configs can list several data folders.

**Model** (`m1.py`; restructured 2026-10-23 at the owner's request, 828,643 parameters):
1. **Encoders, one per input:**
   - **pose:** the motion state, through a two-layer network;
   - **signal groups:** each `MotionSignal` group (intent, conversation, emotion, attention,
     audio, context) has its own small two-layer network. A keyword arrives one-hot, so the
     first layer acts as a learned embedding table.
2. **FiLM:** the signal features scale and shift the pose features, so a signal changes *how*
   she moves. It starts at zero, meaning no effect.
3. **Fusion:** a two-layer network turns everything into the one learned vector the GRU reads.
4. **GRU** (1 × 256), then a two-layer **decoder** whose last layer starts at zero, so an
   untrained model predicts "no change".
5. **Activations:** GELU throughout; the GRU has its own sigmoid and tanh gates.
6. **Signal neutralisation:** in training each signal group is set to neutral for 30% of
   windows, so neutral means "unknown".
7. **Export:** features are selected with fixed matrices rather than indexing, so the export uses
   only operations the runtime spike already ran in Unity. A test checks this and ONNX parity.
8. **Two heads** (roadmap decision 1):
   - `rotation`: a residual on each joint's 6D rotation;
   - `omega`: ω = ω_max · tanh(raw), integrated.
9. **Both heads also predict:**
   - the root's horizontal velocity in her heading frame;
   - the hips height, absolute (integrating a vertical velocity sank the first smoke model 14 m in
     60 s);
   - foot contacts.

**Training** (`scripts/train_m1.py`, config `configs/m1/baseline.json`):
- **Rollout curriculum:** teacher forcing, then rollouts of 8 and then 32 ticks of the model's
  own output, in 72-tick windows with 8 warm-up ticks.
- **Input noise:** 0.03 on recorded inputs.
- **Losses:** geodesic rotation, root-relative joint positions (FK), angular velocity and
  acceleration, root velocity and height, and contacts.
- **Optimiser:** AdamW with a cosine schedule.
- **No gradient through fed-back outputs** (`feedback_gradient`, off by default). The state
  holds a finite-difference angular velocity (×60 per second), and through 32 ticks of feedback
  the gradient reached 4×10⁹ in the first smoke run.
- **Output:** `runs/<name>/` keeps the config, a log, and the best and last checkpoints.

**Evaluation** (`m1_eval.py`, `scripts/evaluate_m1.py`):
- 60 s free rollouts from test-subject seeds, with the neutral signal.
- Measures:
  - stability;
  - joints beyond their limits;
  - ω saturation;
  - planted foot slide;
  - jitter;
  - motion energy per 10 s (freezing or running away);
  - hips height;
  - error against the recording at 0.5, 1 and 2 s.
- Every measure is also taken on the recordings, as the reference.

## Smoke runs (600 steps each, 2026-10-23; 3% of a baseline run)

| | First design (533k) | Current design (829k) | Recordings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable | yes | yes | yes |
| Joints beyond limits | 0% | 1.2% | 1.1% |
| Planted foot slide | 0.93 m/s | 0.36 m/s | 0.11 m/s |
| Jitter | 6,464 m/s³ | 1,720 m/s³ | 499 m/s³ |
| Motion energy over 60 s | 0.30 → 0.28 rad/s | 0.13 → 0.07 rad/s | 1.12 rad/s |
| Hips height (mean, lowest) | 0.84, 0.69 m | 0.43, 0.16 m | 0.78, 0.09 m |
| Error at 0.5 / 1 / 2 s | 12.7° / 17.1° / 18.6° | 11.9° / 14.9° / 16.6° | |

The current design is smoother with less foot slide, but in free rollouts it winds down and
sinks low (towards LAFAN1's ground and falling poses). Too early to judge; the baseline run will
show whether it persists.

**Speed and a crash:**
- **Speed:** the current design trained at about 0.64 s per step (the first: 0.36 s), so a
  20,000-step run is about 3.5 hours. Some of that may have been other GPU use at the time.
- **Crash:** one attempt crashed natively inside a GPU data copy (exit code 9) after 100 steps;
  the rerun was clean. Watch for it on long runs.

## Open decisions (owner)

- **Intent labels.**
  - `MotionSignal` v1's intents (idle, wave, sit, lean, climb, stand, walk, point, look, present)
    have no run, fall or get-up. The roadmap puts fall and get-up among M1's intents, and LAFAN1
    has all three.
  - Extending the list changes a frozen contract, so training uses the neutral signal for now.
  - Each clip keeps its LAFAN1 category (`notes["action"]`) for when the mapping is decided.
- **AMASS and BABEL:** need the owner's registration.
- **The viewer** (a three.js VRM viewer for judging motion): not built yet.
- **Heads and sizes:** the rotation against ω comparison and the size sweep (128, 256 and 512
  wide; 1 or 2 layers) wait for real runs. Size logic (roadmap, owner decision 2026-10-11):
  - **runtime:** the runtime spike measured 3.0M parameters (512 × 2) at 1.13 ms per tick (99th
    percentile) on Sentis CPU in SORA's player, against a 1.67 ms budget;
  - **data:** LAFAN1 and AMASS give roughly 10M frames at 60 Hz, but neighbouring frames are
    nearly identical, so a large model memorises clips;
  - so the sweep picks the smallest model within noise of the best.
