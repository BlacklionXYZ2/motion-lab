# M1: the motion prior

Plan: [the roadmap's M1](../../SORA/docs/plans/MOTION-CORE-ROADMAP_2026-10-11.md).

Since 2026-10-25 every model version is **self-contained** in `versions/vN/` (owner's decision).
Each holds its own code (data conversion, the model, training and evaluation), configs, converted
data, trained runs, outputs and tests. New work happens in the newest version's folder; older
versions stay exactly as they were. Raw downloads are shared in `data/`.

| Version | What it is | Model | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [v0](../versions/v0/README.md) | experiments before v1: the LAFAN1-only baseline, smoke, load and resume tests | `runs/baseline` | kept; no code snapshot |
| [v1](../versions/v1/README.md) | LAFAN1 + 100STYLE + CMU (37 h), bone-direction-matched retargeting | `runs/combined` (the owner's kept model) | evaluated: smooth, but drifts between datasets' neutral poses and winds down |
| [v2](../versions/v2/README.md) | as v1, retargeted relative to each dataset's own T-pose | `runs/combined` | evaluated: datasets now agree; drifts into extreme limb poses in long free runs and is about 10× calmer than the data |
| [v3](../versions/v3/README.md) | v2 plus a quality filter (broken CMU frames, duplicate takes), joint and hips limit losses from the data, and rollouts to 128 ticks | `runs/combined` | evaluated: limb drift fixed (outside limits 21% → 3.6%), but even calmer than v2, and the hips float in 2 of 16 seeds |
| [v4](../versions/v4/README.md) | v3 plus keywords (MotionSignal v2 draft, labels from the datasets) and the hips height above the support | `runs/combined` | evaluated: hips fixed (never above 0.96 m over the support), limits 2.1%, lowest error yet; keywords steer weakly (walk 0.20 m/s against the data's 0.56) and the motion still winds down. The v5 stress battery found more: frozen in every 5-minute run, gliding in 91% of moving frames, hovering, never turning |
| [v5](../versions/v5/README.md) | v4 plus the stress battery, a per-tick latent (learned prior), feet scored in the world, and a velocity input (draft) | `runs/latent-vel-free7` (steers, glides); `runs/latent-vel-lock-sup` (walks on planted feet) | **freezing solved** (latent + free runs + correlated noise); **steering** solved for gliding models (free7: within 10%); **gliding and hovering solved by construction** with the foot and ground locks (lock-sup: carried feet 0.04%, the recordings 3.4%). Open: steering under the lock (lock-sup ignores the command), facing under the lock. `lock-free` and `lock-sup2` training (2026-10-31). 25 runs; see the v5 README |

The full pipeline description, including the history from v0 to v2, is
[versions/v3/docs/M1.md](../versions/v3/docs/M1.md); v4's additions are in its README.
